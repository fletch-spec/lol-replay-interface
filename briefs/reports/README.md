# Reports

One `report-NNN.md` per executed brief, written at Stage 4 by the executing
session from [`../report-template.md`](../report-template.md). Capped at 60
lines. The reviewing session appends its verdict to the bottom.

This file plus `git diff main...brief/NNN` plus three sections of the brief is
the *entire* input to a review. That is the point: judging a brief should not
cost a session's worth of loaded context, and it only avoids that if the report
carries measurements rather than claims.

The Outcome section in an archived brief is written from here, by
[`../REVIEW.md`](../REVIEW.md). Reports stay after the brief ships - an Outcome
is the conclusion, this is the evidence it was drawn from.
