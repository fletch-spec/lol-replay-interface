# Plans

One `plan-NNN.md` per executed brief, written at Stage 1 by the executing session
from [`../plan-template.md`](../plan-template.md). Capped at 25 lines.

This is the cheap gate. It exists so a decomposition can be checked for ~600
tokens instead of a conversational stop into a fully loaded context. A plan that
wants to be longer than the cap is describing a brief that should have been two.

Plans are kept after the brief ships. They are the record of what the executing
session thought the work was *before* it did it, which is the only thing that
makes a report's Deviations section readable later.
